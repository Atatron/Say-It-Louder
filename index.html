When a voice shows up, check two channels.

The brain normally tags inner speech with a copy signal that says “this is me.” In many people who hear voices, that tag is weak, so their own inner speech can sound like someone else. You, Yourself doesn't try to repair the brain's signal. It adds an outside one: a lip-muscle sensor (inside channel) and the phone microphone (outside channel).

Wiring

Power the MyoWare from 3.3 V so its ENV output never exceeds what the ESP32 ADC can take. Use ADC1 pins (32–39), because ADC2 stops working while the radio is on. The motor draws more current than a GPIO can supply, so drive it through an NPN transistor with a flyback diode.
